Miami-Dade Approves "Welcome to Vice City" Sign Deal With Rockstar

Published September 19, 2026 · 3 min read

Miami-Dade County has entered an arrangement allowing Rockstar Games to install a "Welcome to Vice City" sign at the county-owned Kaseya Center, home of the Miami Heat. Rockstar is reportedly paying close to $1 million for the sign, with negotiations reportedly underway for additional signage at other county properties.

Some county commissioners have publicly raised concerns about the arrangement, according to local reporting — this isn't a universally uncontested deal locally.

Why it's notable

This is a real-world marketing spend connecting GTA VI's fictional Vice City directly to actual Miami, months before the game's November 19 launch. Miami-Dade officials have described GTA VI as a potentially major cultural moment for the region this year.

It's also a data point for anyone tracking how far Rockstar is willing to take GTA VI's marketing beyond the game itself — alongside the officially announced 34-track soundtrack album, it's the second major real-world commercial move tied to the launch in the last few days.
