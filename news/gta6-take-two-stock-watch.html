No New Delay, No GTA VI Land — What's Actually Confirmed About Take-Two Right Now

Published September 30, 2026 · 5 min read

Every few weeks, a new claim shows up online about GTA VI's business side — a delay, a new online mode date, "official" virtual land you can buy. We track this so you don't have to sort real news from rumors. Here's where things actually stand as of today.

The one real update this week

A new GTA VI magazine feature went live with developer interviews and 12 new screenshots. That's the marketing beat we were expecting. Nothing else about the launch date or online mode changed alongside it.

What Wall Street is saying about Take-Two

Take-Two Interactive (ticker: TTWO) is the company behind Rockstar Games and GTA VI. On September 28, JPMorgan kept its "Overweight" (buy-leaning) rating on the stock with a $310 price target. That's a reaffirmation of an existing view, not a new upgrade — it didn't change the overall picture much.

Take-Two's own guidance for its next fiscal year (FY2027, which covers most of the GTA VI launch window) still points to roughly $8.0–$8.2 billion in bookings — a term the company uses similar to "sales." Outside analysts' average estimates land close to that too, around $8.49 billion in revenue and about 37 million GTA VI copies sold baked into those numbers.

One way to read how "expensive" a stock is compares its price to its expected earnings — a lower number generally means investors are paying less per dollar of profit. Take-Two is currently trading at roughly 29–32 times its expected earnings, versus about 16 times for the gaming industry as a whole. In plain terms: the stock price already assumes GTA VI is going to be a very big hit. That cuts both ways — it leaves less room for a pleasant surprise, and more room to disappoint if sales come in lower than expected.

The soft spot in the business

Away from GTA VI, some of Take-Two's existing games have cooled off. Spending by players already in its other titles was down about 1% last quarter, mobile game revenue fell 7%, and the company's own guidance expects that ongoing spending to drop roughly 5% this quarter. GTA VI is carrying a lot of the growth story right now.

What to watch next

Take-Two's next earnings report is expected around November 5 — about two weeks before GTA VI launches. That's when the company could update its bookings guidance, confirm or walk back expectations, and give a clearer read on how preorders are going. We'll cover it here.
