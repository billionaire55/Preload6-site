PS5 vs Xbox Series X|S for GTA VI: Which Should You Buy?

Updated for the November 19, 2026 launch · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto VI launches day-and-date on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S — there's no exclusive here. If you already own one, the decision is made. But if you're buying a console specifically for this game, the two platforms aren't identical, and the gap matters more than most comparisons let on.

The short answer

If you're starting from zero and have no console preference, PS5 is the slightly safer default — Sony has positioned itself closely with Rockstar around this launch, and the PS5 install base is larger, which tends to mean better long-term matchmaking for GTA Online. If you already have an Xbox ecosystem (Game Pass, friends, a library), stick with Xbox — the gameplay experience itself is built to be equivalent across both.

Where they actually differ

Storage

GTA VI is a large install. Both platforms let you expand storage, but the process and cost differ — PS5 uses M.2 NVMe SSD expansion, Xbox Series X uses a proprietary expansion card that costs more per gigabyte. If storage is a concern, factor that into the price comparison, not just the console itself.

Console pricing tiers

Both platforms offer a disc-drive and digital-only version at different price points. Since GTA VI's physical edition ships with a download code rather than a disc, the disc drive itself buys you less than it used to — worth weighing if you're deciding between tiers purely on price.

Subscription ecosystem

Xbox Game Pass includes a rotating library and, depending on tier, day-one access to some first-party titles — GTA VI won't be part of that, since it's a third-party release, but it's a factor if you're weighing overall value. PlayStation Plus has its own tiers with a different mix of benefits.
