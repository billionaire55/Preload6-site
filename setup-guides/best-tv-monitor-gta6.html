Best TV & Monitor for GTA VI

Updated for the November 19, 2026 launch · 5 min read

You don't need a new TV or monitor to play GTA VI — but if yours is due for an upgrade anyway, here's what actually matters and two picks depending on where you're playing.

What actually matters

  • HDMI 2.1. This is the one non-negotiable spec — it's what unlocks 4K at 120Hz on PS5 and Xbox Series X. A TV without it caps you at 4K/60 or 1080p/120, no matter what else it does well.
  • VRR (Variable Refresh Rate). Smooths out frame-rate dips instead of showing tearing or stutter — most noticeable in a dense open-world game like GTA VI.
  • Refresh rate. 120Hz is the real target on console; higher numbers on monitors (144Hz, 160Hz+) mostly matter if you're also gaming on PC.
  • Input lag. Look for a dedicated "Game Mode" — it strips out the processing that adds visible delay between button press and on-screen action.

If you're playing on a TV
