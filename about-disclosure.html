What PRELOAD 6 is, and how it's funded

What this site is

PRELOAD 6 is an independent buying guide built around one launch: Grand Theft Auto VI, out November 19, 2026. We write setup guides, hardware comparisons, and launch-week explainers — the practical stuff, not news you've already seen ten times.

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony Interactive Entertainment, or Microsoft. "Grand Theft Auto," "GTA," "PlayStation," and "Xbox" are trademarks of their respective owners.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy something after clicking one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects which products we recommend or how we rank them — our picks are based on our own research and testing criteria, written before any affiliate link is added.

We participate in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.com, along with other retailer affiliate programs as they're added. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

How we pick products

We prioritize products that are actually relevant to GTA VI — storage that matters for the install size, controllers and headsets that hold up to long sessions, displays that make sense for the platforms it ships on. We don't recommend something purely because it pays a higher commission.
